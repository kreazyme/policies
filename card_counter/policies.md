📄 Privacy Policy (Chính sách quyền riêng tư)

Last updated: 08/02/2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bảng điểm bài (Card Counter) (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects, uses, and protects user information.
The app is designed solely for score tracking and game management purposes and does not involve real-money gambling.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

Player names (user-defined)

Game tables and match history

Scores and game rules set by the user

All data is created by the user and remains offline by default.

3. How We Use Information

The stored data is used only to:

Display score history

Manage players and tables

Restore game sessions

We do not:

Track user behavior

Collect analytics without consent

Share data with third parties

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored locally using a local database.

No data is transmitted to external servers unless future features explicitly require it.

Users can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect user data (such as ads or analytics), unless clearly stated in future updates.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not target children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
